  1. Population growth, regional Queensland, 2024-25

    30 June 2025 preliminary estimates; revised 2024 comparison

    Queensland Government Statistician's Office, using ABS Regional population released 31 March 2026. Tables 1 and 2 report Redland City population and annual population increase. These are estimated resident population figures, not Census counts.

  2. Redland 2021 Census All persons QuickStats

    2021 Census, Redland local government area

    Australian Bureau of Statistics. The median age is a 2021 Census measure. It is not presented as a 2026 estimate.

  3. Local Disaster Management Plan, December 2025, pages 31-32

    2024 economic snapshot and 2023-24 industry measures

    Redland City Council, reproducing NIEIR and ABS economic data. Figures 9 and 10 identify NIEIR 2024 estimates for GRP, local jobs and employed residents, and ABS 2024 for businesses. The tourism and agriculture section reports 2023-24 measures. These are dated observations, not live or newly estimated 2026 figures.

  4. City profile, insights and data

    Council's current profile and research hub

    Redland City Council. Provides economic, community, housing and industry profiles and downloadable snapshots. Each linked publication has its own reference period.

  5. Southern Moreton Bay Islands demographic and employment snapshot

    2021 Census

    Redland City Council, using the 2021 ABS Census. The snapshot's Southern Moreton Bay Islands statistical geography includes Coochiemudlo Island. Its figures must not be described as the population of only Russell, Macleay, Lamb and Karragarra islands.

  6. North Stradbroke Island / Minjerribah demographic and employment snapshot

    2021 Census

    Redland City Council, using the 2021 ABS Census. Reports whole-island population, median age and age composition. These are Census observations, not current visitor numbers or 2026 population estimates.

  7. Annual Budget 2026-27

    2026-27 adopted budget

    Redland City Council. Budgeted revenue and capital works are plans, not actual spending. The 5.47% general-rate comparison applies to the median owner-occupied Category 1A property and is separate from utility and other charges.

  8. Budget Publication 2026-2027 (PDF, 29 MB)

    2026-27

    Redland City Council. The full document is linked from Council's annual budget page. Dashboard budget figures are checked against that page's published summary.

  9. Annual Report and Community Financial Report 2025-26

    2025-26, adopted 14 October 2026

    Redland City Council. Links to the latest annual report, financial statements and community financial report, separate from the forward budget.

  10. Council meeting minutes and agendas

    Current official meeting document access

    Redland City Council. Council links to its InfoCouncil register and states that minutes from 2007 onwards are available online.

  11. InfoCouncil meeting document register

    Current official agendas and minutes

    Redland City Council. This is the register linked from Council's minutes and agendas page.

  12. Council meeting livestreaming and video recordings

    Current meeting viewing routes

  13. Redland City Council on YouTube

  14. Council meeting webcast archive

    Recordings from September 2015 onwards

    Redland City Council. The current archive listed the 14 October 2026 General Meeting when checked.

  15. Council meeting schedule

    Official schedule

    Redland City Council. Check Council's schedule for the meeting date, time and location before attending or watching.

  16. About Birkdale Community Precinct

    Planning update following the July 2026 General Meeting

    Redland City Council. Council reports that the whole-of-precinct Local Government Infrastructure Designation was formalised. This is a planning milestone, not a statement that the precinct or whitewater centre is complete.

  17. Capalaba Town Centre: sales suite and project update

    12 March 2026

    Redland City Council, Your Say Redlands Coast. The dated Council update describes a $183 million renewal project. This differs from the undated $250 million figure on Council's RIC overview, so the dashboard identifies the date of the chosen estimate.

  18. Capalaba Town Centre: RIC project overview

    Undated overview, checked 9 November 2026

    Redland City Council. The overview gives a $250 million estimate. The dated 12 March 2026 update gives $183 million. The dashboard identifies both sources without inferring the undated page's age or a final project cost.

  19. Toondah Harbour PDA: current status and next steps

    24 April 2026 update

    Redland City Council. Council acknowledges Walker Group's withdrawal as development partner and describes work with the Queensland Government on future directions.

  20. Weinam Creek Priority Development Area Project

    Status as at 31 July 2026

    Redland City Council. Council reports completed land resumption and a state-led EDQ design tender for the multi-storey car park.

  21. Dunwich Ferry Terminal Upgrade

    Updated 29 September 2026

    Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads. TMR identifies the project as planning and says feedback from May-June 2026 consultation will inform the business case. The page gives different dates for planning and business-case completion, so no completion deadline is inferred here.

  22. 14 October 2026 Council meeting: Cleveland Town Centre update

    14 October 2026

    Redland City Council webcast. Public agenda item 9.2 is Cleveland Town Centre Revitalisation Project Update; a separate confidential update appears at item 19.4. A listed item alone does not establish a project value or delivery milestone.
